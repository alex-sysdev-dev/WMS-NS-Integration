# NetSuite WMS (SuiteApp) — not licensed here, useful as a spec

Every NetSuite WMS topic Alex pulled carries the banner:

> **Warning** — This feature is not currently available in your account. To upgrade, contact your
> Administrator.

So LED Connection does **not** have the NetSuite WMS Advanced SuiteApp. That is the premise of this
project. These docs are still worth keeping because they describe the flows Oracle shipped after
watching real warehouses use NetSuite — a free functional spec for our handheld screens.

One forward-looking constraint: **Intercompany Cross-Subsidiary Fulfillment is documented as incompatible
with NetSuite WMS Advanced** (`fid=section_1515703010`). If LED ever licenses WMS Advanced, cross-subsidiary
fulfillment is off the table, and vice versa.

## Inbound flow the SuiteApp models

`fid=chapter_1541435915` (Inbound Processing) → Receiving Orders, Receiving by Scanning, Receiving Inbound
Shipment Orders, Item Receipts, Item Putaway.

Preconditions it enforces before an order can be received on a device (`fid=section_1541435923`):

- All orders must be approved.
- The order's warehouse location must match the location selected on the device (for transfer orders, the
  **to** location).
- POs and transfer orders must be `Pending Receipt` or `Pending Billing/Partially Received`;
  customer returns must be `Pending Receipt` or `Pending Refund/Partially Received`.
- Outsourced POs are not supported.
- Centralized POs are supported but must carry target subsidiary and target location on the lines;
  centralized POs do not support drop shipments/special orders and do not appear on inbound shipment
  records.

Receiving screen sequence: choose order (scan / select from list / **Find PO By Vendor**, **Find PO by
Item**, **Find PO by MPN**) → enter item (scan name, number, or UPC; `Show Completed` reveals fully
received lines) → item alias if the item has several → autogenerate lot number (optional) → enter lot
(with optional expiry; `Receive All` accepts all expected lots) → enter quantity (inventory status,
restock yes/no for returns, unit selection, optional scale weight capture, optional label print) →
enter serial numbers if serialized → **enter bin**. If receipts are not posted manually, NetSuite WMS
generates the item receipts automatically.

### System rules worth stealing as feature flags

- `Stage received items before putting away?` — receive to a staging bin, put away as a second step.
- `Manually post item receipts?` — receive physically without generating an item receipt yet;
  post later from the app or from `WMS Inbound > Post Item Receipt` (`fid=section_155993562169`).
  One receipt covers everything received for the order since the last posting.
- `Enable Advanced Barcode Scanning?` — GS1, composite, or HIBC barcodes.
- `Enable Tally Scan?` — scan each unit to increment received quantity by 1; optionally across item types.
- `Scan bar codes with bulk serial numbers?` — PO receiving only; up to 300 serials from one barcode.
- `Enable Receive All option for inbound shipments`.
- Autogenerate lot numbers on receipt.
- Capture item weights from a connected scale.
- Restock vs write-off for returned items (only one restock setting can apply to partially received items).

### Inbound shipment receiving

Receiving inbound shipments on a device requires the **Inventory Detail on Inbound Shipment** feature,
which NetSuite Support must provision before it can be enabled (`fid=section_158212308061`). Shipments must
be `In Transit`. GS1 barcodes work; composite barcodes do not. Vendor details from the linked PO are not
visible until an item is entered. Inbound shipments with similar items from different POs must be received
separately, and one item receipt is generated per PO on the shipment.

### Inbound reversal

`WMS Inbound > Inbound Reversal` reverses item receipts generated by mobile receiving — whole line items
only, no partials (`fid=section_1541436036`). Reversal removes the received quantities from the bins they
were received or put away into, and fails rather than creating negative inventory. Reversed receipts are
deleted and the transaction returns to Pending Receipt. **Not supported for item receipts associated with
inbound shipments.**

## What to carry into our own design

- The staging-bin-then-putaway split, and the "post the receipt later" option, are both real warehouse
  needs that Oracle shipped as toggles. Our receiving flow should assume the physical step and the
  accounting step are separable.
- Tally scan (increment per scan) is the right default for a fabrication shop counting loose parts,
  versus typing a quantity.
- Reversal must refuse to go negative. Ours should too.
- Find-by-vendor / find-by-item / find-by-MPN are the three ways floor staff actually locate a PO when
  they are holding a box and not a document.
